Title: Loan Collections Conduct Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-02-08
Category: Collections

Classification: SYNTHETIC / TRAINING ONLY

1. Purpose and Applicability
Define respectful, evidence-based management of overdue fictional accounts. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Collections Office owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Contact attempts must be recorded with date, channel, outcome and owner.
3.2 Collection employees must not use harassment, misleading statements or unauthorized disclosure.
3.3 Payment disputes are referred for investigation before further escalation.
3.4 Hardship requests should be reviewed under the applicable documented process.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 The fictional borrower challenges an amount; staff suspend automated escalation pending verification.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant collections activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
