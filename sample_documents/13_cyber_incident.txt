Title: Cybersecurity Incident Response Plan
Policy ID: DEMO-CY-501
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2026-03-15
Category: Cybersecurity
Document Owner: Security Operations
Classification: SYNTHETIC / TRAINING ONLY

1. Purpose and Applicability
Specify demonstration incident classification and recovery responsibilities. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Security Operations owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Containment actions must preserve relevant logs and avoid destroying evidence.
3.2 Critical simulated incidents are escalated to Security Operations and the Incident Manager immediately.
3.3 Post-incident reports list impact, containment actions, timeline, root-cause uncertainty and lessons learned.
3.4 Access to evidence is restricted to authorized incident response staff.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 An invented credential phishing simulation is isolated from production and documented as training.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant cybersecurity activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
