1. Purpose and Applicability
Provide an internal fictional identity verification standard, not a substitute for applicable law. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Compliance Department owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Verify identity using approved identification material and record the verification method.
3.2 Flag incomplete or inconsistent address information for remediation.
3.3 Periodic review cadence in this synthetic policy is 12 months for high-risk and 36 months for standard-risk profiles.
3.4 Do not open a new account until essential customer identity evidence is present.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A mismatch between submitted name and verified identity is an exception requiring manual review.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
