Category: Lending

1. Purpose and Applicability
Provide front-line employees with a consistent document-verification checklist for loan intake. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Retail Lending owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Confirm applicant age at least 21 and at most 60 at scheduled loan maturity for this invented product.
3.2 Request two independent income evidence sources, such as payslips and verified bank statements.
3.3 Confirm employment or business continuity for at least 12 months, or flag a policy exception.
3.4 Calculate illustrative debt-to-income ratio using verified liabilities and proposed monthly installment.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A self-employed fictional applicant may submit tax summaries and bank credits for review.
4.2 Missing liabilities must be treated as incomplete information, never as zero liabilities.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant lending activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
