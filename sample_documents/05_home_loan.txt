1. Purpose and Applicability
Define fictional housing loan process and collateral documentation. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Mortgage Team owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls

3.2 Illustrative maximum loan-to-value ratio is 80 percent except approved exception cases.
3.3 Legal title review and identity checks are required before disbursement.
3.4 Disbursement is blocked if mandatory property or income evidence is missing.
