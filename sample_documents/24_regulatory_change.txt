1. Purpose and Applicability
Document a fictional workflow to track externally communicated requirements before internal adoption. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Regulatory Change Office owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Assign each proposed change an accountable owner and legal review state.
3.2 Record a source URL or approved legal memo when analyzing actual regulations.
3.3 Keep proposed, approved and implemented internal policy states separate.
3.4 Do not treat AI-generated text as an authoritative regulatory interpretation.

4. Synthetic Case Examples

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant compliance activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
