1. Purpose and Applicability
Describe unsecured personal loan controls used only in the demonstration bank. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Personal Lending owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Illustrative principal range is INR 50,000 to INR 1,000,000 subject to credit risk approval.
3.2 Illustrative tenor range is 12 to 60 months and requires affordability calculation.
3.3 A self-declared salary alone is insufficient; supporting documents must be checked.
3.4 Exceptions require lender notes and manager escalation.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A customer asks for INR 900,000 with only one payslip; request additional evidence before recommendation.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant lending activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
