Title: Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan
Policy ID: DEMO-BC-702
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-30
Category: Continuity
Document Owner: Continuity Office
Classification: SYNTHETIC / TRAINING ONLY

1. Purpose and Applicability
Document response priorities in a fictional branch service disruption. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Continuity Office owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Maintain a list of essential business services and accountable recovery owners.
3.2 Conduct scheduled tabletop tests and record achieved recovery times.
3.3 Escalate test failures into remediation actions with owners.
3.4 Restore customer-facing channels only after integrity and security checks.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A simulated outage exercise measures restoration steps but does not prove production resilience.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant continuity activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
