1. Purpose and Applicability
Provide controls for local generative AI demonstrations that analyze sensitive categories. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Model Risk Office owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Model answers must show source passages, uncertainty and human-review requirements.
3.2 No automated loan approval or adverse customer determination is permitted from a language-model narrative alone.
3.3 Prompt or retrieval failures must be recorded as quality incidents.
3.4 Synthetic documents must be labeled clearly to avoid confusion with operative legal rules.

4. Synthetic Case Examples

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant ai governance activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
