Title: Banking Credit Risk Policy
Policy ID: DEMO-CR-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2025-01-15
Category: Credit Risk
Document Owner: Credit Risk Office
Classification: SYNTHETIC / TRAINING ONLY

1. Purpose and Applicability
Establish a reproducible synthetic framework for reviewing unsecured lending requests. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Credit Risk Office owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Minimum illustrative credit bureau score is 680; cases below 680 must be declined or recorded for exceptional review.
3.2 Monthly total obligations after the proposed loan should not exceed 50 percent of verified gross monthly income.
3.3 Applications above INR 1,500,000 require a second reviewer before final approval.
3.4 Risk analysts must review recent payment delays and income stability before recommending any approval.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A fictional applicant with a score of 700 and a 46 percent debt ratio may proceed to further verification, not automatic approval.
4.2 A score of 665 triggers exceptional review; document the reason and reviewer authorization.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant credit risk activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
