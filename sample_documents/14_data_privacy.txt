1. Purpose and Applicability
Define minimization and access safeguards for synthetic banking records. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Data Protection Office owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Collect the minimum customer information necessary for the documented business purpose.
3.2 Restrict sensitive document access by authorized role and need to know.
3.3 Test exports must use synthetic or anonymized data, not real customer secrets.
3.4 Suspected unauthorized disclosures must be recorded and escalated to the privacy lead.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A development team requests production identities for a demo; decline and offer synthetic substitutes.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant data privacy activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
