1. Purpose and Applicability
Provide controls for selecting and monitoring hypothetical technology vendors. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Vendor Risk owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Perform security and continuity due diligence before onboarding a critical vendor.
3.2 Record access rights and data-processing arrangements in approved contracts.
3.3 Review supplier performance and incidents at established intervals.
3.4 Exit plans should identify data recovery and access revocation steps.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A training vendor storing synthetic support documents requires documented data-retention terms.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant third party risk activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
