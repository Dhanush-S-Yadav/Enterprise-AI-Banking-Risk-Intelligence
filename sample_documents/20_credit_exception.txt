1. Purpose and Applicability
Define recordkeeping for demonstrative exceptions to credit policy. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Credit Governance owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Each exception record must identify the relevant policy version and control threshold.
3.2 Document original assessment, exception rationale, approving officer and approval date.
3.3 Expired exceptions require new review rather than automatic extension.
3.4 Periodic reviews should test whether override patterns are increasing.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A fictional loan exception missing approver details is recorded as an evidence gap.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant credit risk activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
