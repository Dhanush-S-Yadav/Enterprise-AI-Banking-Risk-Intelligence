1. Purpose and Applicability
Describe role-based access control checks in this fictional enterprise. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The IAM Team owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 All privileged access requires documented approval and periodic review.
3.2 Provisioning requests must include the business need, approver and duration.
3.3 Terminated user access must be revoked promptly and exceptions logged.
3.4 Service credentials must not be embedded in application source code.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 The test auditor finds a stale administrative role and requests immediate review.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant cybersecurity activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
