Title: Operational Risk Management Framework

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-02-01
Category: Operational Risk

1. Purpose and Applicability
Define fictional risk identification, assessment and mitigation tracking. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Operational Risk Office owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Risks are assessed on a demonstrative 1-to-5 likelihood and impact scale.
3.2 High-priority items require a named owner, target date, and independent closure validation.
3.3 Control failures must be categorized by process, people, technology or external events.
3.4 Residual risk assessments must include rationale and documented supporting evidence.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A fictional outage with likelihood 3 and impact 4 gets illustrative risk score 12, not a predictive real-world loss amount.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant operational risk activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
