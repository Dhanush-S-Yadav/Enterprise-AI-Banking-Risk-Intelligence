1. Purpose and Applicability
Specify the minimum fictional onboarding and review evidence. This standard is internal to the imaginary Atlas Demo Bank and must not be treated as law, a real bank policy, or advice to customers.

2. Roles and Responsibilities
The Customer Assurance owns the document and maintains an evidence trail. Front-line personnel gather records, reviewers validate conditions, and managers approve documented exceptions according to their role.

3. Required Controls
3.1 Confirm customer identity and stated purpose of account relationship.
3.2 Record a risk classification with rationale and review it when material changes are observed.
3.3 For enhanced due diligence, document source-of-funds questions and reviewer approval.
3.4 Missing source-of-funds documents are a missing-evidence finding, not proof of wrongdoing.

4. Synthetic Case Examples
4.1 A fictional business customer with unusual ownership structure is referred for enhanced due diligence.

5. Evidence and Audit Requirements
For all relevant compliance activities, retain the case identifier, document version, reviewer, date, control result, evidence reference, and any exception rationale. A missing document is an evidence gap; it does not prove control compliance or misconduct.

6. Escalation and Human Judgment
Where a condition is unclear, employees must seek review by the designated owner. The example thresholds are invented for a learning project and cannot be used to decide actual banking services.

7. Limitations
This document is synthetic. It does not represent an actual bank, regulator, statute, customer account, production incident, or live transaction. Any AI-generated summary must cite this source and acknowledge missing evidence.
